Chile’s 2022 Platform Work Law requires digital platforms to monitor the formality of their independent contractors, and sets maximum working hours, minimum pay, and the right to unionize for these workers. Chile combines unique datasets for platform work with rampant informality in this sector, reaching around 80.0 percent before the reform. DiD and event-study designs show the reform led platforms to replace 9.6 percent of informal contractors with formal ones, without any effect on the employee share, wages, or compliance with other regulations. A structural model of imperfect compliance with labor regulations in the platform sector helps interpret these findings.
